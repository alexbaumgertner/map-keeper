# OSM community obligations

## Before launch

- [ ] Post on community.openstreetmap.org describing Mapkeeper (compliance-as-a-service, not bulk editor)
- [ ] Conversation with DWG before production edits
- [ ] OSM wiki project page (Organised Editing Guidelines)
- [ ] Public contact for complaints (response within 24 hours)
- [ ] Register `taginfo.json` with taginfo.openstreetmap.org

## Product stance

- Edits under the user’s own OSM account only
- Human confirmation before every write
- No bulk/chain edits in MVP
- Name/domain must not contain “OSM” or “OpenStreetMap”
